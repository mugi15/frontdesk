# Frontdesk submission note

Replies are posted in the customer portal. Email delivery and delivery receipts were left out so the audit can state exactly what the demo verifies.

The hosted review uses a shared reviewer password with an agent selected at sign-in. Customers track requests in the browser where they created them. Individual staff accounts and cross-device customer accounts are beyond the demo.

The holiday calendar contains a sample date. Arvo's actual holidays would need to be supplied. On Vercel, idle tickets are closed by a daily job. Opening the inbox or portal checks for expired tickets sooner, and a late customer reply is blocked at the cutoff.
